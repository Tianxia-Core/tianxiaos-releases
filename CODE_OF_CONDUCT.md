# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **TianxiaOS** and **Rasa-X-Machina** engineering community a harassment-free, technically rigorous, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive, high-trust engineering environment include:
- Grounding technical feedback in reproducible benchmarks, ledger telemetry, and formal verification evidence
- Respecting differing viewpoints, languages, and global markets (42 supported jurisdictions)
- Accepting constructive architectural critique gracefully
- Prioritizing user privacy (`GDPR++`), accessibility (`WCAG 2.2 AA`), and system safety

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information or credentials without explicit permission
- Introducing backdoors, obfuscated dependencies, or intentional Build Law bypasses

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to project leadership at `coltak@rasaxmachina.com`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
